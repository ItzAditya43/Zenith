Zenith

A private AI chat app that runs entirely on your own computer — no account, no subscription, nothing sent to a company's servers.

Most AI chat apps send everything you type to a company's servers, somewhere you can't see. Zenith doesn't. Everything — the chat, your files, your notes — stays on the computer it's running on.

You do need one thing: Ollama, a free program that runs AI models directly on your computer. Zenith is the friendly chat app on top of it.

What it can do

💬 Chat

Ask it anything, attach photos or documents, have it read a webpage for you.

🧠 Remembers you

Learns preferences and facts you mention over time, so you don't repeat yourself.

📝 Notes & to-dos

A built-in notes and task list, and you can just ask it "what's on my to-do list?"

🛠️ Can take action (optional)

With your explicit permission, it can run commands, edit files, and use git — you approve every step, or set an autonomy level you trust.

📱 Any device

Desktop app for Windows/Linux, or open it in a browser on your phone.

🆓 No subscription

Free and open source. The only cost is your own computer's electricity.
